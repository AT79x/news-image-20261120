## 1. SHENZHEN — Trump, Xi and Putin sign a peace declaration (hero story)
- US President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian
  President Vladimir Putin met in Shenzhen on Thursday 19 Nov on the
  sidelines of the APEC summit — the first ever trilateral meeting of the
  leaders of the world's three largest nuclear powers — and issued a
  four-point "Declaration toward Peace".
- The four points: reform the United Nations; increase production and supply
  of refined petroleum products and safeguard stable energy flows; respect
  for state consent and official immunity, warning the International
  Criminal Court against abusing its jurisdiction; and a dialogue on
  reducing nuclear risks, reaffirming that a nuclear war cannot be won and
  must never be fought. The text does not mention Ukraine, Iran or North
  Korea, and sets no date for the nuclear dialogue. The meeting lasted
  about 80 minutes.

## 2. THE HAGUE — world court: Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya
- The International Court of Justice ruled on Thursday 19 Nov, by nine
  votes to six, that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in
  northern Rakhine State in 2017 — the first time in the court's 80 years
  that it has held a state responsible for genocide, and only the fourth
  time since the Holocaust that an international court found the legal
  threshold for genocide met.
- Presiding Judge Yuji Iwasawa: Myanmar's security forces killed thousands
  of Rohingya civilians, perpetrated widespread sexual violence and extreme
  violence against children, and attacked those fleeing to Bangladesh. The
  case was brought by The Gambia in 2019; the ruling is final, with no
  appeal. More than 700,000 Rohingya fled to Bangladesh in 2017.

## 4. GAZA — Israeli raid ends, Salah al-Din road reopens
- Israel's military said on Thursday it had concluded a 24-hour tank raid
  to dismantle a suspected Hamas tunnel in central Gaza, west of the
